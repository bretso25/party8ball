Format: Double Elimination — every team needs two losses to be eliminated. Each division has a Winners Bracket (WB) and a Losers Bracket (LB). The WB champion faces the LB champion in the Grand Final. With 10 tables available, multiple matches run simultaneously each night — keeping things moving without marathon sessions.
